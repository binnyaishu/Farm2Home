# Farm2Home
Fresh Food Purchase
Farm2Home is an online platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, enabling users to purchase fresh farm produce from the comfort of their homes. The platform aims to eliminate middlemen, ensure fair prices for farmers, and deliver fresh, high-quality products to customers.

📌 Features:
🧑‍🌾 Direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace
🥦 Browse fresh fruits, vegetables, and farm products
🛒 Easy online ordering system
🚚 Home delivery of farm-fresh produce
💰 Transparent pricing
🔐 Secure and simple checkout process

🎯 Use Case:
Farm2Home is useful for:
Consumers looking for fresh and organic produce. 
Farmers wanting to sell directly to customers. 
Supporting local agriculture and sustainable farming.
